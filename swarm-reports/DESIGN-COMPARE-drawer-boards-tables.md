## Verified NOT gaps

- **Drawer closes three ways + focus trap.** X (`DrawerHeader.jsx:61`), Escape (`TaskDrawer.jsx:684`), scrim click (`TaskDrawer.jsx:666`). `ui/FocusTrap.jsx` traps Tab, restores focus to the trigger, and falls back to `[data-focus-fallback]`/`<main>` when the trigger unmounted. Exit animation is driven by `animationend` on the resolved keyframe name, not a literal duration.
- **Kanban touch drag.** 04 §3's "HTML5 drag does not fire on touch" does not hold — `@hello-pangea/dnd` has been in force since `KanbanView.jsx:3`, which also supplies Space-to-lift, arrow-key column moves and live-region announcements (9.1 a11y).
- **Single-call move.** `PATCH /tasks/:id/move` with `{column_id, order}` (`KanbanView.jsx:387`), `flushSync`, optimistic `.pending` at `opacity .6`, full-record rollback, `.just` settle flash.
- **3-state sort, resize, indeterminate select-all, shift-range.** `nextSort` + `.tbl__sort` reserved-width SVG (`TableView.jsx:88-101`), pointer-event resize with `setPointerCapture` (`tableHooks.js:59-79`), `Checkbox mixed` → `aria-checked="mixed"` (`TableView.jsx:221-226`), anchor-based shift range (`tableHooks.js:110-126`).
- **Field-visibility reset and the `<details>` menu.** Both fixed — visibility reconciles by id in `localStorage` (`useBoardView.js:141-168`), the panel is `ui/Popover` (`BoardToolbar.jsx:48`).
- **Ordered grouping.** `views/grouping.js` + `PRIORITY_RANK`, not `Object.entries`.
- **URL serialisation.** Search, filter, group and sort are query params, `{replace:true}` (`useBoardView.js:119-139`) — 10.1/10.4 satisfied for the views; only the drawer tab is missing (M10).
- **Status-colour drift.** One map, `lib/statusColors.js`; the drawer's and StatusChip's local maps are gone.
- **Inline `:hover` mutation, approval-pill literals, `--k-danger`, avatar index conditional.** All resolved (`TaskCard.jsx:9-27`, `boards.css:336-341`).
- **Tailwind.** Zero Tailwind utilities anywhere in scope.
- **Inline style objects.** Confined to genuinely computed per-instance values — `{'--c': color}`, `{width: pct}`, `{width: widths[key]}`. The exceptions are M11 (`ActivityList.jsx`), M12 (`ActivityFeedPage.jsx`) and `views/CalendarView.jsx` (416 lines, ~35 inline objects) — but 04 §5 explicitly holds Calendar back for a design pass, so it is out of scope rather than a gap.
- **Bilingual pairing.** `--font-hindi` for fixed designer strings, `--font-indic` for language-following labels, `letter-spacing: 0` + `text-transform: none` resets present where tracking could inherit (`boards.css:78-86`, `drawer.css:170-184`, `components.css:47-50`). Weight is 400 throughout. Only contrast is wrong (M16, `.apv-seg__hi`).
- **Dark mode.** Everything in `boards.css`/`drawer.css` is token-driven, so no `[data-theme]` block is needed. The three real dark-mode defects are the hardcoded `#fff` on token fills (H7, H8, L5).
