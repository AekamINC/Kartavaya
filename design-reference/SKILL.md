Key brand elements:
- Warm paper canvas (#F6F3EC), not white or gray
- Newsreader serif for display, Inter for UI, Tiro Devanagari Hindi for bilingual labels
- Blue→Teal gradient accent (#0082c6 → #03a1b6 → #05b7aa)
- Dark sidebar (#050E1A) with bilingual nav labels
- Every heading/section/nav item has a paired Devanagari label
- No emoji, no decorative illustrations — text-forward editorial design
