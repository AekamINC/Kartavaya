The three corrections

The silhouette is a star. No outer circle at all — the last course of petals defines the boundary, and alternating long and short points give the edge its rhythm. This is the single biggest difference and I had it wrong in every version until now.

Petals are flames, not lenses. Round at the base, widest at about 40% out, drawn to a point at the tip. A vesica is symmetrical and reads as a leaf; a flame reads as a lotus petal.

Petals contain petals. Each carries a smaller flame inset within it and a dot at its base — the nested interior is what makes a line drawing look drawn rather than plotted.
