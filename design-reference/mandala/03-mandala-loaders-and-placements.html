Mandala — the loader, and the eight places it earns its keep

All four loaders keep क at the centre and build the mandala around it, so the mark stays the subject and the ornament stays ornament. Below that: where mandala work belongs elsewhere in the product, and — just as important — where it does not.

Loader — क at the centre

Geometry is generated, not hand-drawn: petal counts, radii and angles come from a few numbers, so a ring can be retuned without redrawing anything.

Where mandala belongs

Ranked by how much the ornament actually does. The test each one has to pass: does it make the screen easier to read, or is it decoration competing with the work? Green tags are worth building; amber are worth trying but need care.

Where it does not

Behind data. Tables, ledgers, task lists, the Kanban board. Anything with rows and figures needs an even, quiet ground — a pattern under a column of rupee amounts makes them harder to scan, and scanning is the entire job of those screens.

Behind form fields. Same reason, plus contrast: input borders and focus rings have to stay the most legible edges on screen.

On every module tile. Fifteen modules each with their own mandala is a pattern library, not an identity. One shared motif used sparingly reads as considered; fifteen read as a theme.

In dark mode at the same opacity. A line that sits at 7% on cream disappears at 7% on near-black. Every placement below needs its own dark value, not an inherited one.
