Rich mandala क — sixteen courses, four motions

The earlier mandalas had three rings and it showed. This has sixteen courses built from a vocabulary — vesica petals, graduated dot rings, scalloped lace, teardrops, spikes, hairline circles — alternating type as they go out, the way the drawings do. क sits in the eye of it and never moves; everything else does. Same figure in all four, only the motion changes.

Four motions, one mandala

All 260-odd elements are generated from ring definitions, so a course changes by editing a number rather than by redrawing a path.

The ring vocabulary

Sixteen courses, outward: hairline eye · 8 filled petals · 8 petal outlines with echo · 16 graduated dots · 16 petals with echo · 24 scallops · 24 teardrops · 32 graduated dots · 32 fine petals · double hairline · 16 large petals with echo · 48 scallops · 48 spikes · 48 dot lace · closing hairline.

Offset nesting throughout — every course is rotated half a step against the one beneath, so petals sit in the gaps. It is most of the difference between a flower icon and a mandala.

Weight hierarchy — structural circles heaviest, petal outlines mid, dot lace lightest. A drawing where every line has the same weight reads as clip art no matter how many rings it has.
