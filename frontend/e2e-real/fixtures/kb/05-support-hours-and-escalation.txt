Zanskar Medtech Limited — Support Hours and Escalation

Business hours
    Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 hours India Standard Time.
    Saturday, 09:00 to 13:00 hours, for Gold plan customers only.
    Closed on national holidays and on the second Saturday of each month.

Contact
    Email     support@zanskar-medtech.example
    Portal    the customer portal, which raises a ticket automatically
    Telephone +44 7700 900500 (a reserved drama number, not a real line)

Ticket priorities
    P1  Instrument down, patient testing stopped.
        First response 1 hour. Target resolution 8 working hours.
    P2  Instrument degraded, testing continues with a workaround.
        First response 4 hours. Target resolution 3 working days.
    P3  Question, request or cosmetic fault.
        First response 1 working day. Target resolution 10 working days.

Escalation path
    Level 1   Support engineer on the ticket
    Level 2   Regional service manager, after 2 breached targets or on request
    Level 3   Head of Customer Operations, Farhan Sheikh
    Level 4   Managing Director, Suresh Pillai

A P1 ticket that is unresolved after 24 hours escalates to Level 3
automatically, without the customer having to ask.

Loan instruments
    A loan instrument is offered to Gold plan customers when a repair is
    expected to exceed 72 hours. It is despatched within 1 working day of the
    decision and is returned within 7 days of the repaired instrument being
    recommissioned.
