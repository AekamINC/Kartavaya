Zanskar Medtech Limited — Warranty and Returns

Standard warranty
-----------------
Every ZM-series analyser carries a standard warranty of 24 months from the date
of commissioning, or 30 months from the date of despatch, whichever expires
first. The warranty covers parts and labour for defects in materials and
workmanship. It does not cover consumables, calibration drift caused by use
outside the stated environmental range, or damage from an unauthorised repair.

Extended warranty
-----------------
An extended warranty of a further 24 months may be purchased at any time before
the standard warranty expires. The price is 11 per cent of the invoiced
equipment value per year. It cannot be bought retrospectively once the standard
warranty has lapsed.

Returns
-------
Goods may be returned within 21 days of delivery if they are unopened and in
original packaging. A restocking charge of 15 per cent applies to any return
that is not the result of a despatch error on our part. Consumables, reagents
and calibration standards cannot be returned once the tamper seal is broken.

RMA numbers
-----------
No return is accepted without a Return Material Authorisation number. RMA
numbers take the form ZM-RMA-YYYY-NNNN, for example ZM-RMA-2026-0417. Request
one from support@zanskar-medtech.example. An RMA number is valid for 30 days
from issue.
