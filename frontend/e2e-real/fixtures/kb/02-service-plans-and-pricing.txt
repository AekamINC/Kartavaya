Zanskar Medtech Limited — Service Plans

We offer three service plans. All prices exclude GST and are per instrument per
year.

Bronze — Rs. 38,000 per year
    One preventive maintenance visit per year.
    Remote diagnostic support during business hours.
    On-site response within 5 working days.
    Parts charged separately at catalogue price.

Silver — Rs. 74,500 per year
    Two preventive maintenance visits per year.
    Remote diagnostic support during business hours.
    On-site response within 2 working days.
    Parts included up to Rs. 40,000 per year.

Gold — Rs. 1,42,000 per year
    Four preventive maintenance visits per year.
    Remote diagnostic support 24 hours, every day.
    On-site response within 1 working day, including Saturdays.
    All parts included with no annual cap.
    A loan instrument if a repair exceeds 72 hours.

Plans are billed annually in advance. A plan may be upgraded at any point in the
year on payment of the difference, pro-rated to the day. A plan may not be
downgraded mid-term.

Instruments older than 9 years are eligible for Bronze only.

Multi-instrument discount: 7 per cent for 3 to 9 instruments on one site, and
12 per cent for 10 or more.
