Zanskar Medtech Limited — Data Retention and Privacy

This note explains what we hold, why, and for how long. It is written for
customers who are asked by their own auditors.

What we hold
    Service records, including the serial number, the fault reported, the parts
    fitted and the engineer's notes.
    Contact details for the named laboratory contacts at each site.
    Remote diagnostic logs, which contain instrument telemetry only.

What we do not hold
    We hold no patient data of any kind. Remote diagnostic logs are stripped of
    sample identifiers at the instrument before transmission, and the
    instrument will refuse to transmit if that stripping fails.

Retention periods
    Service records                       8 years from the date of the visit
    Remote diagnostic logs                13 months
    Contact details                       for the life of the contract plus 1 year
    CCTV at the Mysuru works              30 days
    Recorded support calls                90 days

Legal basis and rights
    Personal data is processed to perform the service contract. A data
    principal may request access, correction or erasure by writing to
    privacy@zanskar-medtech.example. We respond within 30 days.

Sub-processors
    Remote diagnostics are hosted in the Mumbai region of our cloud provider.
    No customer data leaves India.

Breach notification
    We notify affected customers within 72 hours of becoming aware of a
    personal data breach, and the regulator as required.
