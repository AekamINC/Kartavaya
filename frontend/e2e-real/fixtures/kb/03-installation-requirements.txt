Zanskar Medtech Limited — Site and Installation Requirements

Prepare the site before the engineer arrives. An engineer who finds the site
unready will re-schedule, and a wasted visit is charged at Rs. 6,500.

Electrical
    Dedicated 230 V single-phase supply, 50 Hz, 16 A, on its own breaker.
    Earth-to-neutral voltage must be below 2 V.
    An online UPS of at least 3 kVA is required for the ZM-900 and ZM-1200.

Space and clearance
    Bench depth of at least 750 mm.
    Clearance of 300 mm at the rear and 150 mm on each side for airflow.
    Bench must support 140 kg for the ZM-1200 and 65 kg for the ZM-500.

Environment
    Ambient temperature 18 to 28 degrees Celsius.
    Relative humidity 30 to 70 per cent, non-condensing.
    Not in direct sunlight and not within 2 metres of an air-conditioning outlet.

Water and drainage
    Type II deionised water, resistivity above 10 megohm-centimetre.
    A gravity drain no more than 1.5 metres from the instrument.

Network
    One RJ45 port with a static IPv4 address on the laboratory VLAN.
    Outbound TCP 443 to the update service. No inbound ports are needed.

Installation takes one working day for the ZM-500 and two for the ZM-900 and
ZM-1200, plus a half day of operator training.
