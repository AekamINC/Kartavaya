Zanskar Medtech Limited — Commissioning Checklist for the ZM-900 Analyser

This checklist is completed by the commissioning engineer and countersigned by
the laboratory manager. A copy is left on site and a copy is filed against the
instrument serial number. It is deliberately long, because an instrument that
is commissioned badly generates service calls for its whole life.

Section A. Before the engineer travels
The order acknowledgement must name a site contact and a delivery date. The
site readiness form must have been returned and must confirm the electrical
supply, the bench loading and the water quality described in the site
requirements note. The engineer confirms that the instrument serial number on
the despatch note matches the number on the purchase order. Any mismatch is
resolved before travel, because a serial number corrected after commissioning
propagates into the service history and cannot be unpicked cleanly.

Section B. On arrival
Inspect the outer packaging for crush damage, punctures and tilt indicators.
Photograph any damage before the packaging is opened. Confirm that the shock
indicator on the crate has not tripped. A tripped indicator does not stop
commissioning, but it is recorded and photographed, and the customer is told
before the crate is opened rather than afterwards.

Section C. Physical installation
Position the instrument on the bench with the specified clearances. Level the
instrument using the four adjustable feet and confirm with a spirit level on
both axes. Connect the deionised water supply and the gravity drain, and check
every union for leaks under running conditions for five minutes. Connect the
waste container and confirm that the level sensor reads correctly when the
container is empty and when it is half full.

Section D. Electrical and network
Confirm the supply voltage under load and record it on the checklist. Confirm
that earth to neutral is below two volts. Connect the instrument to the
uninterruptible power supply, not directly to the wall socket. Assign the
static address supplied by the laboratory and confirm that the instrument
reaches the update service on outbound port four four three. Confirm that no
inbound port has been opened, because none is required and an open inbound port
is a finding at the customer's next audit.

Section E. Fluidics and optics
Prime the fluidics and run the automatic wash cycle three times. Inspect the
sample probe for burrs and confirm the depth setting. Run the optical
self-test and record the lamp energy figure. A lamp energy below the minimum
stated in the service manual is replaced during commissioning and not left for
the first service visit.

Section F. Calibration and verification
Run the calibration set supplied with the instrument. Record every calibrator
lot number on the checklist. Run the verification controls at two levels and
confirm that both fall within the stated acceptance range. If either fails,
repeat the calibration once. If it fails a second time, stop and escalate to
the regional service manager rather than adjusting the acceptance range.

Section G. Operator training
Train at least two named operators. Training covers daily start-up, the wash
and shutdown routine, changing the sample probe, replacing the waste diaphragm
kit, reading the error codes, and raising a support ticket. Record the names
and the duration of the training on the checklist. Leave the quick reference
card on the bench.

Section H. Handover
Walk the laboratory manager through the checklist item by item. Both parties
sign. Record the commissioning date, because the twenty four month standard
warranty runs from that date and not from the date of despatch. Upload the
signed checklist to the customer portal within two working days. The warranty
is not activated until the signed checklist is uploaded.

This document is a test fixture. Zanskar Medtech Limited does not exist.
