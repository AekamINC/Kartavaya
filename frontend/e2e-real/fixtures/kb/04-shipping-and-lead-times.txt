Zanskar Medtech Limited — Despatch and Lead Times

Lead times are counted from the date a purchase order is acknowledged, not from
the date it is received.

Instruments
    ZM-500      ex-stock, despatched within 4 working days
    ZM-900      built to order, 6 weeks
    ZM-1200     built to order, 11 weeks
    ZM-1200X    built to order, 16 weeks

Consumables and reagents
    Despatched within 2 working days if ordered before 15:00 hours.
    Cold-chain reagents are despatched Monday to Wednesday only, so that no
    consignment is in transit over a weekend.

Transit times from the Mysuru works
    Karnataka and Tamil Nadu          1 to 2 days
    Maharashtra, Telangana, Kerala    2 to 3 days
    Gujarat, Rajasthan, Delhi NCR     3 to 5 days
    North-east states                 6 to 9 days

Freight
    Orders above Rs. 2,00,000 are despatched freight prepaid. Below that,
    freight is charged at actual with no handling markup.

Partial despatch
    We do not part-ship an instrument order. Consumable orders are part-shipped
    on request at no extra charge.

Damage in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with
photographs of the packaging before it is discarded. A claim made after 48
hours cannot be supported with the carrier.
