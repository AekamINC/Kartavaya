# doc status

## Also called
document status, invoice status, draft, final, issued, locked, frozen,
edit invoice, edit the invoice, editable, amend, revise, correct an invoice,
एडिट, संशोधन, फाइनल, ड्राफ्ट, लॉक

## What it means here
Whether an invoice or a quotation can still be changed is decided by PAYMENT,
not by status. Anything with no payment recorded against it can be edited and
sent again, whatever its status says, and sending a copy to the customer does
not freeze it either. The word "final" is only the value a document is created
with — it does not mean issued, approved or locked, and nearly every document in
the product carries it without anybody having chosen it. Recording a payment is
what freezes the figures, and after that the remedy is a credit note.

## A wrong answer looks like
"That invoice is marked final, so it can no longer be edited." Final is the
default. With no payment recorded, Edit is available and the invoice can be
corrected and re-sent — and telling somebody otherwise leaves them with a
document that can never be fixed and never be issued.
