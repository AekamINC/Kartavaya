# gstin

## Also called
gst number, gst no, gstin number, pan, pan number, tan, tan number, tax number,
tax id,
जीएसटी, जीएसटीआईएन, जीएसटी नंबर, पैन, टैन

## What it means here
GSTIN, PAN and TAN are optional fields, and they block nothing at all. A client,
a contact, a vendor or the firm's own profile saves perfectly well with all
three left empty, and nothing in the product refuses to proceed because one is
missing — not adding a client, not raising a quotation, not creating a sales
order, not raising a tax invoice, not producing its PDF, not sending anything.
The firm's OWN number missing from a tax invoice, credit note or debit note is
reported to staff as an advisory in the invoice drawer, because a supplier who
IS registered fails e-invoice validation without it and the recipient's input
tax credit is blocked. Advisory is all it is: a supplier below the registration
threshold legitimately has none, the document is produced either way, and the
PDF simply omits the line where the number would go rather than warning about
it on a page the customer reads.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Add the client's GSTIN first, then you can save them." The field is optional
and saving is not blocked. So is "you cannot generate the tax invoice PDF until
your own GSTIN is filled in" — it generates, with that line left off. Telling
someone a GSTIN is mandatory sends them chasing paperwork the software never
asked for, on a record that would have saved and a document that would have
gone out as it was.
