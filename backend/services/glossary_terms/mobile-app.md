## What it means here
The mobile app is a companion to the browser, not the whole product. Invoices
are read-only on it: they can be looked at, not created, edited or sent. Signing
is not on it at all. Anything that issues a document, moves money or decides
money is done in a browser.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Edit the invoice from the app." There is no edit there, so the honest answer is
to open it in a browser. Sending somebody hunting for a button that was never
built costs them the trip and reads as a broken product.
