# contact

## Also called
contacts, contact person, point of contact, poc, contact details,
कॉन्टैक्ट, कांटेक्ट, संपर्क, संपर्क व्यक्ति

## What it means here
A contact is a PERSON at a client company — a name, a phone number, an email.
Contacts belong to a client and one client usually has several. They are the
part that turns over: a contact leaving means the client needs a new contact, it
does not mean the client is gone.

## A wrong answer looks like
"You have 240 customers" when what was counted is contacts. One company
routinely holds three or four of them, so counting people and calling them
customers overstates the customer base every time — and the number then
disagrees with every other number the firm has.
