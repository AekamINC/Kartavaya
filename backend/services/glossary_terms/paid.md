## What it means here
An invoice becomes paid only when money is seen arriving in the bank: a
statement line is matched against the invoice, or a receipt is recorded by hand
by somebody who checked. There is no payment gateway in this product and there
is not going to be one. A pay page, a UPI address and a QR code tell a customer
where to send money — none of them reports back that it arrived, so nothing
marks itself paid on its own.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Once the customer pays through the link, the invoice will update
automatically." It will not. Somebody reconciles the bank statement or records
the receipt, and until then the invoice is unpaid no matter what the customer
has already done at their end.
