# module names

## Also called
graha, ganit, vikray, manav, vetana, pahchan, prachar, sanvaad, varta, dristi,
srijan

## What it means here
The sections of this product carry Sanskrit names. Each one is a part of the
software, not a general word: Graha is CRM — clients, contacts and deals. Ganit
is finance — invoices, payments and GST. Vikray is sales — quotations and sales
orders. Manav is HR. Vetana is payroll. Pahchan is attendance. Prachar is
marketing. Sanvaad is internal messaging between people in the firm. Varta is
WhatsApp to the outside world. Dristi is analytics and reports. Srijan is the AI
and skills section this assistant belongs to.

## A wrong answer looks like
Translating the name instead of using it — "graha means planet or house in
Sanskrit, so this may relate to astrology". Nobody asking about Graha is asking
about astrology. They are asking about the CRM, and every sentence after that
guess is about the wrong subject.
