# esign

## Also called
e-sign, e sign, digital signature, electronic signature, sign, signature,
signing, sign a document, aadhaar esign,
ईसाइन, ई-साइन, साइन, हस्ताक्षर, सिग्नेचर, डिजिटल हस्ताक्षर

## What it means here
Signing here is in-house and deliberately not Aadhaar-based. A document is
prepared, sent to a signer, and comes back with an audit trail — who signed,
from where and when — plus a hash of the file, which is what makes a later
alteration detectable.
It is a web feature only. The mobile app is not somewhere a document is sent for
signature or signed, and that is a decision rather than a gap waiting to be
filled.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Open the app and sign it there", or "you will need an Aadhaar OTP to e-sign".
Neither is true. Signing happens in a browser, and no Aadhaar number is ever
asked for.
