# sales customer

## Also called
sales customers, customer list, customer master, customer record, add a customer,
create a customer, new customer,
सेल्स कस्टमर, कस्टमर लिस्ट, ग्राहक सूची, नया ग्राहक, नया कस्टमर

## What it means here
There is no separate customer record to create and no customer list anybody
maintains. The client company IS the customer; it is simply marked as a sales
customer the first time a quotation or a sales order is raised against it. So
the customer list is a view of the clients that have ordered — it is produced by
the orders, not kept alongside them.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Go to Sales and add the customer there first." There is nowhere to add one, so
that instruction ends at a screen that does not exist. Pick or create the client,
raise the order, and the customer appears because of the order.
