# upi

## Also called
upi id, upi ids, vpa, virtual payment address, upi handle, upi qr, paytm,
phonepe, gpay, google pay,
यूपीआई, वीपीए, पेटीएम, फोनपे, गूगल पे, क्यूआर कोड

## What it means here
A firm holds a separate UPI address per platform — one for Paytm, one for
PhonePe, one for Google Pay — because each settles into its own account, and one
of them is marked as the default that a pay page and its QR use. It is not a
single UPI field holding one value.
Nothing validates a UPI address, and nothing can: a wrong character does not
fail, it quietly pays whoever does hold that handle, with no callback and
nothing to reverse. Scanning the QR preview is the only check that exists,
because it shows the account holder's name as their bank reports it.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Enter your UPI ID in settings", as though there were one — ask which platform.
And never reassure anybody that a mistyped UPI address will bounce or be
rejected. It will not, and that answer is how money leaves for good.
