# client

## Also called
customer, clients, customers, account, accounts, buyer, party,
क्लाइंट, क्लायंट, ग्राहक, ग्राहकों, कस्टमर, पार्टी

## What it means here
A client is the COMPANY that buys from this firm. It is one record and the same
record everywhere it appears — in CRM, on a quotation, on a sales order, on an
invoice, on a signature request. The people who work at that company are
contacts, and they are a different thing: contacts join, leave and get replaced,
while the client stays and keeps its whole history.

## A wrong answer looks like
"Your client Ramesh Patel has two overdue invoices." Ramesh is a person, so he
is a contact; the client is the company he works for. The same error the other
way round is saying a client was lost because the person who dealt with them
left — the company is still the client, it needs a new contact.
