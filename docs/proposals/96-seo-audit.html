01

Executive summary

Assessment: critical

Kartavaya's SEO problem is not invisibility. It is misidentification. Google has already crawled, indexed and formed a view of the site — and that view is two product generations out of date.

Correction to the earlier read

My first pass framed this as “Google cannot see the product.” A live SERP check disproves that: www.kartavaya.com is indexed and does surface — as “Kartavaya — by Aekam Inc… team task management software.” That is the stale production build being served back as your public identity, including inside AI-generated search summaries.

This makes the redeploy more urgent, not less. You are not starting from zero; you are correcting an established record, which is slower.

Biggest strength

The positioning itself, once deployed. GST built in, not bolted on and the client approves in one click are specific, defensible and true — and the four legal pages (privacy, security, DPA, sub-processors) are roughly a thousand words each of exactly the substance a cautious buyer reads. Most early-stage competitors have nothing comparable.

The three priorities that matter

  • Redeploy production. Every other item compounds off this. Until it lands, all SEO work optimises a page that isn't public.
  • Win a brand query you can actually win. Bare “Kartavaya” is lost to a 1995 Bollywood film and a common personal name — see §5. Target Kartavaya Aekam and Kartavaya software, and build the entity signals that disambiguate.
  • Publish more than one page. 614 words total is not a site. The category is won with comparison and guide content, which is precisely how every competitor ranks today.

Finexo, QwikCA, Vider, Papilio and PracticeStacks all rank for category terms via their own “Top 10 / Best… 2026” articles. Kartavaya publishes none of this. That is the single largest gap, and it is a content gap rather than a technical one.

05

The brand-name problem

verified on SERP

Owning your own name is normally the one free win in SEO. Here it is not free, and the reason is worth understanding before spending anything on it. 

Query: "Kartavaya" — top results, live:
  1. Kartavaya Gupta — LinkedIn (a person)
  2. @travelwithkartavaya — Instagram (a person)
  3. Kartavirya Arjuna — Wikipedia
  4. Kartavya (1995 film) — Wikipedia
  5. tiktok.com/tag/kartavaya
  ...
  kartavaya.com does not appear

Query: "Kartavaya Aekam practice management software":
  3. Kartavaya — by Aekam Inc  ← indexed, stale title
  • The name collides with a Bollywood film and a common personal name. कर्तव्य means duty; it is a name people are given. Bare-brand search will stay contested for a long time, and no on-page fix changes that.
  • The winnable brand queries are qualified ones — Kartavaya Aekam, Kartavaya software, Kartavaya practice management. Target those and treat bare Kartavaya as a long game.
  • There is an entity-confusion risk. Aekam Inc reads publicly as a branding and digital-marketing agency. Without Organization schema and a distinct company presence, Google has little reason to model Kartavaya as a software product rather than an agency's page.
  • What actually moves this: Organization and SoftwareApplication schema, a Google Business Profile, a LinkedIn company page, directory listings, and consistent naming as Kartavaya by Aekam Inc everywhere. Entity signals, not keywords.

The strategic read

Kartavaya loses every SEO dimension and wins most product dimensions. That is a good position to be in, because the losing column is the fixable one. The competitors are not out-engineering you; they are out-publishing you.

One genuine product gap: competitors automate filing — ITR, TDS, UDIN and DSC tracking. Kartavaya does GST invoicing and summaries but does not file. Expect that in comparison conversations, and answer it deliberately rather than being surprised by it.
