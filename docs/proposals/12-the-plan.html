The one risk worth repeating. Staging and production share a single database. Every migration in this plan — the channel colour column, the rename's ten rows, the seat model — is a production change the moment it runs. That is why the rename is three passes and not one, and why nothing here touches data before the code that reads it is already deployed.
What I would actually do first. Items 3, 4 and 6 together are under two days and turn the assistant from "free model, grounding off, empty index, no way in" into something worth showing. But item 1 is a leak you have already described as able to lose a customer on the spot, and item 2 has to land before memory or it becomes a bigger leak.

So: 1 → 3 → 4 → 6 → 5 → 2, then the rename, then the look. That front-loads the security work and still has a demonstrably better Sahayak inside the first week.
