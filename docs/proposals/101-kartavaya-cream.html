The accent is doing the work. Pick any of the twelve swatches in the top bar. The hero band, the glass tiles, the brand mark, the bars, the pipeline, the rail, the mesh behind the cream and every accent-tinted shadow retint together — from deriveAccentColors(), the function that already ships in lib/accent.js.

That is the part that makes this more than a paint job: every client org already picks an accent, so every org gets its own identity for free. Saffron for a firm in Ahmedabad, Indigo for one in Bengaluru — same product, same code, one setting.

And Design → Shipping renders the identical markup in today's language. Nothing here is a one-way door.

What makes it bold without going dark. Three things, none of which is a new colour system. Scale — the hero is Newsreader 200 at 72px with −.035em tracking; the shipping build never goes above 40px, and scale is most of what reads as premium. One vivid zone — the KPI band carries --k-grad and everything else stays cream, so glass finally has a ground worth blurring and the eye has one clear destination. The widened ramp — surfaces run #FFFEFB to #DDD6C2 instead of collapsing at 1.017:1 between --bg and --s-low, so elevation actually reads.

What deliberately stayed calm. The receivables table has no glass, no gradient and no blur — flat cream, tabular numerals, semantic pills in their own hues, --row-h intact. Ninety rupee figures each landing on a different contrast ratio is unfixable, and holding the data quiet is exactly what lets the band be loud.

Companions: Motion and Material (the audit and the measurements), Glass Kit (components and the four OS dialects), Kartavaya Dark (the same screen if you ever want a dark-first theme).
