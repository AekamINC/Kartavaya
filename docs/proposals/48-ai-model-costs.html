4 · The two costs that are not the model

Search (Serper). $0.10 per thousand queries, 2,500 free a month. At 20,000 answers a month where a third reach the web, that is ~6,700 queries, ~4,200 of them billable: $0.42 a month. This is noise, and it is the decision that made it noise — Gemini's own grounding bills $14–35 per thousand queries, which at the same volume would be $94–$235.

Images. $0.036 per Gemini image, $0.040 per Seedream image. One image costs more than 150 chat answers. This is already 79% of everything you have ever spent on AI, from 47 pictures. If a cost control is worth building anywhere, it is here — not on the chatbot.

5 · What I would take from this

  • Chat model choice is not a cost decision. The whole realistic range — flash-lite to qwen3.6-plus — is $2.79 to $6.30 a month at five thousand answers. Choose on answer quality and latency; the money is a rounding error against one Railway environment.
  • Latency is the real constraint, not price. qwen3.6-flash averages 15.5s and qwen3.6-plus 24.2s on content jobs, against Gemini flash-lite's 2.1s on chat. Those jobs write ~2,000 tokens and chat writes 273, so it is not a fair comparison — but you already said Sahayak feels sluggish, and switching to a slower model without measuring it on real questions would make that worse, not better.
  • Deep-thinking is affordable if it stays rare. Even Gemini 2.5 Pro on a 30k-token deep run is 5 cents. A "research this properly" button that fires a premium model on demand costs less than the images already do — as long as it is a button and not the default.
  • Fix the unpriced models first. Any billing or per-org cost report built on hub_ai_logs today undercounts every direct-Gemini call on a model missing from the table.
