What is not covered

The three PNGs — favicon.png, apple-touch-icon.png, kartavaya-mark.png — still carry the old diamond. They are now reached only by older Safari and the Android home-screen icon, because the SVG favicon is listed first and every modern browser takes it. kartavaya-mark.png has no consumer left at all.
