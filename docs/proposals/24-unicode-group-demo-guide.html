A running order that holds together

  1. Ganit → Invoices. Open on money. 48 invoices, ₹19,80,900, and an Overdue tile that reads 10 rather than 0 — it counted a status nothing ever wrote until this week.
  2. Ganit → Today / dashboard. The same receivables figure on the landing screen, so the numbers agree across two surfaces. Agreement is the thing a finance buyer is watching for.
  3. Pahchan → Register. 566 punches. Pick a day with a missing clock-out and show the correction request — including, now, the organisation's answer coming back.
  4. Vetana → Payslips. Eight people, 37 slips, downloadable as PDF.
  5. Sanvaad. Six channels, each with its own colour; own messages right, others left.
  6. Sign in as the member account. The same screens with less on them. This is the whole permissions argument and it needs no explanation once they see it.

Three things not to click

Do not send a Prachar campaign. Staging has OUTBOUND_MODE=dry, so nothing leaves the building — but production does NOT, and outbound.py:148 reads an unset value as live. If this demo ever runs against production, a campaign send is a real send. Show the campaign builder and the audience; do not press Send.
Do not expect scheduled things to happen. Eleven cron endpoints have working handlers and no Railway service yet, so drip sequences, scheduled reports and attendance summaries will not fire on a timer during a demo. Everything manual works. Nothing that says "we'll do this for you later" will do it later — not yet.
Do not open the Ganit contracts tab. It is empty, and an empty tab after ten full ones is the one screen that reads as unfinished. Sign something from e-Sign instead.

What changed this week, if it comes up

  • The Overdue tile counts correctly. It counted a status nothing in the product ever wrote — Unicode Group's ten overdue invoices, worth ₹2,98,068, displayed as "nothing past due".
  • Attendance corrections are two-way. An employee can raise one and now see the answer.
  • Files go to object storage. Uploads no longer land in the database as base64.
  • Marketing mail carries an unsubscribe link. It did not before, which was a legal exposure as much as a missing feature.
  • Cross-tenant isolation. Every finding in the leak map is closed — a platform account can no longer reach another organisation's data. Worth saying plainly if you are asked how one customer's data is kept from another's.
