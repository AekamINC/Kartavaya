What Prachar
Is For

Two sweeps. Twelve lenses over the code and the live database, eight more over the world outside it. The internal sweep found the module mostly honest about what it cannot measure. The external sweep found something nobody in this repo had asked: the ICAI Code of Ethics names email as a prohibited way for a chartered accountant to approach someone who is not yet a client.

Which means the fix is not a feature. It is a predicate — one the database already carries and the audience filter does not read.

Read-only throughout. No file changed, no row written. Every live figure below is a SELECT against schema staging on 20 Aug 2026, and every outside claim carries a source.

Prachar sends email, and the email spine works. A campaign resolves an audience from CRM contacts, subtracts the unsubscribe list, and sends through Resend with an unsubscribe link in every message. That is real, and it is more than most half-built modules have.

Everything past the send is missing. There is no webhook, no pixel, no click redirect — nothing in the product receives a delivery event, so opens, clicks and bounces are unmeasured. The module is honest about this: the API nulls the four engagement columns and the screens read Not measured rather than drawing a confident zero. That was the right call and it should not be undone.

The number that reframes everything

There is no CA firm on this platform today. The three live orgs are Aekam Inc, the E2E test org, and Unicode Group — which the seed file describes as a commerce chain, not a practice. Every ethics finding below is therefore prospective. That is the cheapest possible moment to discover it: the gate has to exist before the first CA sale, not after.

Folio 1

The line ICAI draws

the finding no code review would surface

Clause (6) of Part I of the First Schedule to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 makes soliciting professional work — by circular, advertisement, personal communication or any other means — professional misconduct. The Code of Ethics 2026 spells out the modern form: a member may not address letters, emails or circulars to persons likely to require a chartered accountant's services.

The exposure is the customer's, not Aekam's. The software vendor is not a member of the Institute and is not liable. The partner who pressed Send is. That asymmetry is precisely why it is worth engineering around — the risk is invisible from our side of the table and career-shaped on theirs.

It is enforced rather than dormant. ICAI's Board of Discipline has reprimanded a member for soliciting through a WhatsApp pamphlet advertising free accounts, tax, GST and audit services — the closest available analogue to a Prachar campaign. ICAI found 241 members guilty in FY2024-25 against 91 the year before; penalties run to ₹5,00,000 and removal for up to five years.

Do not build on the "ICAI allows advertising now" headlines

The 13th edition took effect 1 April 2026 and was widely reported as a liberalisation. It is — on the pull side. Websites and social pages may now run in push mode. But the same chapter separately bars circulating information about services exclusively reserved for CAs by email except on a specific pull request, and separately bars issuing material that solicits people to visit the firm's website. A grep of the whole of Volume I finds "Push" exactly twice, both about websites. The email prohibition survived the revision untouched. Renaming the module changes nothing — Clause (6) reaches solicitation by any other means.

The permitted side is real, and Prachar already has the join

The Code permits greeting cards and invitations carrying the firm name provided they go only to clients, relatives and friends. The prohibition is scoped to persons likely to require services — prospecting, not servicing. So the line is client versus prospect, and it is a line this repo already models:

Read the fourth row again

The retainer-offer campaign is a draft whose body text says, in the field that gets emailed, that pricing is not confirmed and it must not be sent until the partners agree the bands. The only thing standing between that campaign and a customer's inbox is a sentence inside the payload. That is the whole problem in one row.

One incidental mercy: all four seeds carry segment and seed keys that are not in the allowed filter set, so the filter validator rejects them and the send path calls that validator. The seeded demo campaigns are currently unsendable — by luck, not design.

The other two institutes

ICSI is materially more permissive — its 2020 guidelines permit professional updates and write-ups in any mode, including WhatsApp. Prachar is defensible to a CS firm in a way it is not to a CA firm. One flag: ICSI bars aggregator listing and names JustDial, and this repo has a JustDial lead-ingest path. ICMAI converged with ICAI in guidelines effective 10 April 2026. All three now share one axis; build to it once.

Two renderers, and four variables that ship raw

armed, idle, one click from live

The send path substitutes exactly three variables. Live customer templates use seven. The four that cannot be filled reach the recipient with the braces intact — and the template editor tells the author they were detected.

The divergence is real and was found by reading all three renderers side by side. routers/prachar.py:679 substitutes name, email and company. campaign_sender.py:164 and sequence_step_executor.py:257 substitute {{name}} and nothing else. The same campaign renders differently depending on whether a human pressed Send or the scheduler did. A comment in the interactive path claims it uses the same split as the cron path. It does not.

How live is this, exactly

We settled it rather than guessing. /cron/marketing is armed — it runs daily inside the cron-daily service, confirmed from the Railway start command. outbound_mode is live, confirmed from /api/health. But there are 0 scheduled campaigns and 0 active enrolments, so the cron fires daily and finds nothing to do.

Stated precisely: armed, firing, idle. Nothing broken has shipped yet. The first time somebody clicks Schedule, it will.

The editor makes it worse rather than better. The Templates tab extracts any {{word}} by regex and reports "Merge fields detected", listing tokens the send path has never heard of. Its own comment says the readout exists so a typo does not land in somebody's inbox — but it validates spelling, not support, so it affirmatively confirms a field that will ship raw. Intersecting the extracted set against the supported set is a three-line fix.

Folio 4

A write that crosses tenants

the one thing to fix regardless

Everything else in this document is a product decision. This one is a bug, it is a write, and it should be fixed whatever you decide about the rest.

POST /sequences/{id}/pause runs two statements. The first is correctly scoped to the caller's org. The second has no org filter at all:

Any authenticated user with the Prachar module can pause another organisation's live drip enrolments by posting that org's sequence id. The victim's sequence still reads active, so nothing looks wrong from their side. And because no resume path exists anywhere in the product — nothing ever sets an enrolment back to active — those contacts never receive another step. Setting the sequence back to active does not bring them back.

No victims today: all 8 live enrolments are already completed. Same shape as the graha_clients join leak already on record — scoped on the parent, unscoped on the child.

There is no Indian statute requiring an unsubscribe link or a postal address in email. TRAI's UCC regime does not reach email at all. Rows 2 and 4 are mailbox-provider requirements, not law — which makes them no less binding, because the penalty is silent delivery failure rather than a notice.

A 7.5× spread, and the cheap category is the one ICAI permits. A GST deadline reminder to an existing client is a utility template. A promotional sentence in the same message makes Meta reclassify it as marketing and the price multiplies. The ethics and the economics point the same way.

A 15-person firm messaging 300 clients four times a month — 1,200 messages — costs ₹163/month all-utility, ₹1,222 all-marketing, ₹269 on a 90/10 blend. Trivially affordable. The pricing conclusion

The entire Indian CA practice-management category clears at ₹52–₹150 per user per month for everything. Zoho sells more email capability than Prachar has for ₹315. Prachar cannot be a paid module. It should be a bundled retention feature at ₹0, with WhatsApp sends metered through the credit ledger as the only monetised surface.

And the reason no Indian CA rival ships a marketing module is not oversight — they all ship compliance reminders instead. That is the category. That is where Prachar should compete.

Email infrastructure

At 50,000 messages a month, SES costs about $5 and Resend $20. Price is not a reason to move. The real differences: Resend stores account data in the US whatever sending region you pick, and its suppression list is team-wide across every tenant domain. SES has had per-tenant suppression lists since June 2026. Neither requires Prachar to build a pixel, a click redirect, or a suppression list — all three are provider features. Apple Mail is now about 62% of measured opens and prefetches everything, so open rate is not worth building. Bounces and complaints are.

Folio 7

What to cut

the recommendation nobody else will give
Cut outright
✕

The Events tab

cut50 rows, all in the test org

Not because ICAI forbids webinars — informational ones are permitted. Because the one capability that would make a firm-level event tool structurally different is unreachable, and the commodity is cheap.

CPE credit flows only through ICAI Programme Organising Units; the firm-level exception needs 7+ partners, and of roughly 1,00,138 registered CA firms only 2,129 have even six. The audience for a client webinar are business owners, for whom CPE means nothing anyway. Zoho Meeting sells registration, reminders and polls for ₹1,680/host/month. And the feature was never finished: the public cannot register — the endpoint sits behind a login and an org check.

✕

The Automations tab — already cut, finish the job

cut0 rows, ever

Already unmounted; the POST returns 501 and the list endpoint reports a null engine. The table has held zero rows for the product's entire life. Delete the component and the table — or leave it exactly as it is. Do not build a journey builder against Zoho's ₹855.

✕

SMS, permanently

cut12 undeliverable drafts

Promotional SMS from India needs DLT principal-entity registration, a registered header and registered content templates — roughly ₹5,000–5,900 one-time per firm, 7–21 business days, a three-variable cap. Per-tenant onboarding cost that will never be recovered.

Do not build — the research is unanimous

A tracking pixel. A suppression list from scratch. Landing pages, pop-ups, heatmaps, lead scoring, sequence branching, A/B splits. RCS — no published India price, per-brand carrier approval, and SMS fallback drags you back into DLT. And sub-day drip scheduling: step delays are whole days by column type, so the daily cron constrains nothing. Pin it to a 09:00–10:00 IST window and stop.

Keep the Ads tab. It is a read-only Meta spend reporter, not an ad-placement tool — an earlier reading of mine had that wrong. For a CA-firm tenant it will simply be empty. Not an ethics problem, and not worth deleting working code over.

and why this order
1

The two unsubscribe headers

hoursthe only live defect

Add List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post to the Resend params and the SES branch. Everything downstream already exists — the token, the HTTPS endpoint, the link helper.

First because it is the only item that is a defect today rather than a future one. Gmail has been issuing rejections since November 2025.

2

Fix the merge-field lie, and reconcile the three renderers

hourscustomers already told you

Intersect the editor's extracted tokens against the supported set and render the rest as a warning, not a confirmation. Then make the cron and drip paths substitute the same set as the interactive one.

Your customers have already told you what this product should be by the variables they typed: {{invoice_no}}, {{amount}}, {{due_date}}, {{month}}. That is a transactional set, not a marketing one. It is also a hard prerequisite for WhatsApp, where a parameter mismatch is an API rejection rather than an embarrassment.

3

The cross-tenant write

minutescorrectness

Add the org filter to the second UPDATE in pause_sequence, and add a resume path so pausing stops being a one-way door.

4

Apply migration 107, then 110

not engineeringshared DB

107 removes the invented opens and clicks from a live customer's rows. 110 lets marketing leave a different address from payslips and password resets — the fix for row 3 of Folio 5, already written. Both touch the shared staging/production schema; risk report before either runs.

5

Consent as an append-only event log

daysdue 13 May 2027

consent_status, consent_given_at, consent_source, consent_purpose, notice_version, withdrawn_at on contacts. A boolean cannot discharge a burden of proof; an event log can.

Prerequisite for three separate things: DPDP by May 2027, Meta's opt-in rule for any WhatsApp channel, and the SES production-access attestation — which asks you to affirm you only mail people who requested it and that you have a bounce process. Both of those affirmations would be untrue today.

6

The ICAI audience gate

daysgates the first CA sale

client_id IS NOT NULL as an audience predicate, default on, with prospect sends refused — modelled on the existing non-email channel refusal, which is a good pattern: it names the harm and says what to do instead. Replace the four template categories with ICAI's actual axis (services exclusive to CAs versus everything else), and add a save-time linter for superlatives, comparisons, guarantees and "visit our website".

Sixth rather than first, deliberately. It is the highest-consequence finding in this document, but there is no CA firm on the platform — so it gates the first sale rather than protecting an existing customer. It must land before that sale. A compliance-minded partner will raise it during evaluation whether or not it is fixed.

7

The delivery-event receiver — bounces and complaints only

daysnot opens

Build it on Resend now rather than waiting for any SES decision; the receiver is provider-shaped, not provider-locked. The join key exists: outbound_log.provider_message_id beside detail->>'ref' = 'campaign:<uuid>', and the WhatsApp webhook is a working signature-verified template. Set tags carrying campaign and contact id at send time so the receiver is idempotent.

A drip sends N times per contact where a campaign sends once, so step count multiplies an unsuppressed bounce. Skip opens — Apple has made them meaningless.

8

WhatsApp — as utility notifications, not Prachar campaigns

the wedgethe only priceable surface

Change the WhatsApp template category default from MARKETING to UTILITY — today every template a firm creates defaults to 7.5× the price and to the one category ICAI forbids. Submit templates to Meta and persist Meta's returned category, not the requested one. Wire the status webhook so silently dropped messages become visible.

And do not put it in Prachar. Drive it from the statute calendar and invoice state Kartavya already holds. Prachar's model is audience → one blast; a compliance reminder is per-client, per-registration and date-computed. Wrong data model, right company.

A correction to my own earlier reading

I told you earlier that WhatsApp cannot deliver, citing a comment in the Prachar router. That comment is out of date — the Meta Cloud API call ships and sits behind the outbound gate. Prachar still refuses non-email channels, so the campaign path is genuinely blocked, but the underlying channel works. Three of the twelve internal lenses repeated the stale comment; the repo-grounded pass caught it.

What we could not settle

and what would settle it

Stated plainly, because a research document that hides its gaps is worth less than one that names them.

?

The client/prospect line is reasoning by analogy, not a sourced rule

ICAI's text permits greeting cards and invitations to existing clients. Extending that to deadline reminders and newsletters is an inference — a sound one, but an inference. The Ethical Standards Board's FAQ compilation could not be retrieved by any lens and may answer it directly.

This is the single highest-value unresolved question in the bundle, because folios 1 and 8 both rest on it. The ESB is reachable directly by email and phone. One question, one answer, and the gate design is either confirmed or corrected. A conservative compliance partner may not accept an analogy.

?

Meta's INR rate card was never seen on a Meta-hosted page

Every rupee figure in Folio 6 is corroborated to four decimals across four vendor pages, but Meta serves the card behind Billing Hub. Pull the real card from a Business Manager account before any price reaches a customer. Same for the 1 October service-message rates, unpublished until 1 September.

?

"WhatsApp beats email for Indian CA firms" is vendor-led

There is no rigorous survey. The conclusion rests on convergent vendor behaviour — every Indian CA suite found ships WhatsApp compliance reminders and none ships email campaigns — plus WhatsApp's scale in India. Strong circumstantial evidence, not measurement. Do not put a response-rate number in a deck.

?

Half the internal sweep is unverified

The twelve-lens sweep produced 101 findings and an adversarial refuter killed exactly half of those it reached before the run was paused. The findings in this document are the ones re-verified by hand against the code and the live database. The remainder sit in the salvage file and should be treated as leads, not facts.

Folio 10

The shape of it

one paragraph

Prachar was built as a B2C email marketing tool and sold into a market where outbound marketing is professional misconduct. That sounds fatal and is not, because the same firms have a genuine, dense, recurring communication need — roughly 150 per-client dated obligations a year, about 25 of them broadcastable — and the product already contains the right templates, the right calendar and the right client record. It is pointed the wrong way.

Turn it around. Two tabs earn their place today — Templates and Sequences. Events and Automations should go. The audience filter needs one predicate it does not have. The variables customers actually typed are transactional, not promotional. And the channel the market has standardised on costs ₹0.115 a message in exactly the category the ethics permit.

None of that is a rewrite. It is a smaller product than the one that is there now, aimed at something real.
