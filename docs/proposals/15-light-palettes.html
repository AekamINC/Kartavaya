What ships today, for reference. Not "cheap" in itself — the flatness came from missing depth and structure, not from the colour. With bubbles and shadows it holds up better than it did.
My pick: 4 · Slate, with 2 · Paper as the safe alternative. Slate has the firmest ink and the deepest teal, which matters because a client demo usually runs on a projector or a laptop nobody has calibrated — that is exactly where 1 goes washy and 3 goes green. Paper is the one that never looks wrong anywhere, at the cost of not looking like anything in particular.

Worth saying plainly: option 6 shows the current cream with the new structure, and it is noticeably better than what ships today. The flatness you reacted to was missing bubbles, shadows and colour identity — not the beige. So "keep cream, take the depth" is still a real option, and the cheapest of the six.
