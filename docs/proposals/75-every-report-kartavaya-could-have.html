Every report Kartavaya could have

Nine parallel sweeps across every module, then a second pass that re-ran the numbers. The finding is consistent and slightly embarrassing: the rows are nearly all there. What is missing is the named, findable, exportable list. This is the whole catalogue, with what is real, what is one column away, and what cannot be honest yet.

21 August 2026 · nine module sweeps · every count re-verified live before publication · staging and production share one Supabase instance · read-only throughout

176 of 251 tables are empty. That ratio is the honest answer to “what reports can we have”: the product has far more schema than data, and the useful catalogue is smaller and better than the table list suggests.

What changed while this was being written

The framework was built, worked, rendered to CSV/XLSX/PDF, and was unplugged at both ends — one definition existed and no route could reach it. It is now wired, and five registers landed with it, each reconciled to the rupee against live data. So the numbers below are a snapshot of a moving thing, not a wish list.

Three CRM reports are printing wrong numbers today

  • “Avg days in stage” is the deal's total age. AVG(updated_at − created_at), labelled as time-in-stage. There is no stage-entry timestamp anywhere.
  • “Won value” measures deals created in the window, not won in it. On Unicode Group: ₹7.0L reported against ₹12.0L actual — 42% short. won_at is filled on all 34 wins and unused.
  • The sales leaderboard shows 0% for people who may have sold. 20 of 34 targets key on an employee id rather than a user id, so a 0% means “wrong kind of id” and the name renders blank.

Roughly 4,100 lines of tested read-API over empty tables, and no router imports any of it. This is not a wiring job — it is a feature nobody has built the input side of. Saying so plainly is more useful than five empty screens.

The exception, and it is a real one: the statute calendar — 45 rows of dated law that no router served until tonight. It now has an endpoint, and the endpoint proves its own worth: the same query at two dates returns different law, because six TDS and TCS form numbers change on 1 April 2026.

Three I would not build

  • Anything called a P&L or a trial balance. There is no ledger. The honest product is a register.
  • GST return annexures. place_of_supply holds '27' on 253 rows, 'Gujarat' on 76, and nothing on 52. Normalising that is a data decision, not a report decision.
  • Project profitability. There are zero projects, and the cost side is 0% attributable — it is strictly worse than the client grain, which is itself not yet honest.
