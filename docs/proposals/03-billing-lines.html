Why a table and not more columns

Today there is one column, monthly_price. Adding support_price, then setup_fee, then whatever comes next means a migration every time you invent a way to charge — and one more place the total can disagree with itself.

One row per charge: kind, description, amount, monthly or one-off, start, optional end. The sixth revenue line you think of is a row, not a migration.
