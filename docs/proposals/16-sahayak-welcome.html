My pick: E, with set 1. It is A's warmth plus the one sentence that answers the question a cautious buyer actually has — can my junior ask this about salaries? Saying "I answer from what you can open" turns the RBAC work into a feature the client can see, rather than a promise they have to take on faith.

If you want the demo to land harder in the first three seconds, B is the one — live counts are the fastest way to prove it already knows the business. Its cost is that the counts must always be right, so it needs real numbers rather than a hard-coded line.
