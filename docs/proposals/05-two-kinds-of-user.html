Two kinds of user

Your model, drawn out — and what already exists for it.

1 · Two seat pools, counted separately

ORG SEATS · THE PLAN
12
People who use the product — partners, staff, admins.
  • Every module they're granted
  • Tasks, invoices, payroll, CRM
  • Attendance too — free
PAHCHAN SEATS · THE ADD-ON
200
People who only clock in — site staff, field workers.
  • Attendance
  • Their own payslip
  • Nothing else
An org member never consumes a Pahchan seat. They already paid for a full seat, so attendance comes with it. Only attendance-only people count against the Pahchan pool — and they are the cheap, numerous ones.

2 · What that does to a bill

A CA firm with 12 staff and a client site with 200 workers clocking in.

Without the split, that firm needs a 212-seat plan — priced for 212 people using the whole product, when 200 of them only tap a button twice a day. The split is what makes attendance sellable at all.

3 · Why this unlocks email-optional

Employee gets a Pahchan seatThey install the app to clock in — a daily habit, not a favour.
That seat is an accountWhich links to their employee record.
Payslip becomes self-serviceThey open the app they already use. No email needed.
Payroll email defaults to OFFThe 960 emails that ate your SES quota were delivering something already available.
