UAT & usability testing

Proposal 104 · 2026-08-30 · the two disciplines the testing pass could not automate, written as artefacts to run rather than plans to admire.

Neither of these has ever been done. The QA audit of 2026-08-30 closed nine gaps in code — cross-browser and cross-platform, accessibility, performance budgets, visual/style regression, i18n, mutation testing, dependency scanning, and disaster-recovery verification. It could not close these two, because UAT needs a customer and usability testing needs a stranger. Every row in the product's status ledger that says ✅ today was signed off by the people who built it.

Why this matters more here than it usually does

CLAUDE.md already carries the rule: ✅ means a customer can complete the flow end to end, proven by a row appearing where there were zero — not "the code shipped". That rule was written after proposals 84–90 kept re-deriving the same status because code-without-data had been marked done.

UAT is that rule applied to a person rather than a row. A seeded row proves the write path works. It does not prove a chartered accountant in Ahmedabad can find the button, understands what it will do, and trusts the result enough to send it to a client. Only a customer doing it proves that, and today the E2E suite drives our own test orgs — "E2E Test & Associates", "Unicode Group", "UK AekamINC" — all of which we created, seeded and know the shape of.

Part A — User acceptance testing

Who signs

One practising customer per module family, not an employee and not the owner. The minimum credible panel is three firms: one CA practice (Ganit, Vetana, compliance), one services firm using the PM core (Tasks, Boards, Graha), and one firm with field staff (Pahchan attendance, Vikray orders). Each signs only for what they actually use — a signature over a module nobody touched is worse than no signature, because it reads as coverage.

The rule for every row below

A row passes when the customer completed it unaided, on their own data, on their own device, and the resulting row exists in the database. Not "we showed them". Not "it worked when we tried it". If they asked how, the row is a PARTIAL and the question is the finding.

Part B — Usability testing

UAT asks "can it be done?". Usability testing asks "can it be done by someone who has never seen it, without help, and would they do it again?". The second question has never been asked about this product.

Method

  • Five participants. Five finds the large majority of severe issues; the sixth mostly re-finds what the first five already showed.
  • None of them have seen Kartavaya. Anyone who has watched a demo is spent.
  • Their own device, their own browser. The cross-browser matrix now covers seven engines mechanically — this is where the human half of that lands.
  • Think aloud. Do not help. The silence when someone is stuck is the finding. Time to first success, and every point of hesitation, are the measures.
  • 45 minutes, recorded with consent.

The five tasks

#Given only this instruction…What it is really measuring
U1"Set up your firm here and add one colleague."Whether onboarding is self-service at all. Where they stop is the drop-off point in the real funnel.
U2"Raise an invoice for ₹40,000 to a client called Mehta & Co, and send it."The single most valuable path in the product. Count every hesitation before the send.
U3"Find out which of your invoices are unpaid and how much you are owed."Whether the numbers are findable, and whether they believe them.
U4"Someone new is joining on Monday. Give them access to the Mehta project and nothing else."Permissions comprehensibility. The 4-tier role model is correct and has never been explained to a stranger.
U5"Do U2 again on your phone."The mobile story, with a real thumb. Invoices are read-only on mobile — watch whether that reads as a limit or as broken.

What is already known, and must not be re-discovered by a participant

Wasting a participant on a defect we already have on record is wasting one of five. Fix or flag these before the sessions:

  • The Skill-pack step editor does not work in Safari or at phone width — do not schedule a Safari participant on a skills task.
  • "Save as draft instead" leaves the gate banner up in Safari.
  • The dashboard shows Sanskrit and Hindi to a user who chose English, and Hindi to one who chose Gujarati.
  • Every module page jumps from h1 to h3, so a screen-reader participant cannot navigate by structure.
  • /api/teams is fetched up to four times per page load — U3 and U5 will feel it on a slow connection.

What this proposal asks for

  1. Three customer firms willing to sign Part A, and a date.
  2. Five strangers for Part B, and someone other than the builder to run it.
  3. Agreement that until Part A is signed, no row in docs/STATUS.md claims customer acceptance — ✅ there continues to mean "a row exists", which is a real and different claim, honestly stated.
