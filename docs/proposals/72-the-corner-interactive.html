The thing the demo shows that the proposal could not

Skills cover 7 modules. Automations cover 11. Metrics cover 14. Click through eSign, Marketing and Payroll and you will see it: the tab that is empty is never the same tab twice. Put all four in one dock and there is no page in the product where the corner has nothing true to say — which is exactly what a single-purpose skills dock could not promise.

What is fabricated here, precisely

  • Every result. "4 candidate pairs", "₹2.4L overdue" — invented. No skill has been run.
  • The counts in the pill are the real catalogue counts, but a live dock would also subtract what the caller is not entitled to run.
  • The 19 ganit skills are listed top-5-then-more. The real ordering rule (type, then recency, then name) is not decided yet — that is a question for you, not an implementation detail.
  • Due dates are computed from real due_day values against 20 Aug 2026. The real one must respect effective_from / effective_to — the statute table is dated law and a date read without its window is how you print last year's rule.

Three decisions this demo exists to settle

  • Tabs or one merged list? Tabs are shown. A merged list ranked by urgency — a due date beating a skill beating a metric — is the other shape, and it is a better product if the ranking is right and a worse one if it is not.
  • Does the pill show a count, and of what? A count invites a badge, a badge invites "unread", and the corner becomes a second inbox. Shown here as a plain catalogue count that never nags.
  • Does "Automate" arm anything from the corner? The demo previews the rule and stops. Arming from a corner popover is how a firm ends up emailing its customers by accident.
