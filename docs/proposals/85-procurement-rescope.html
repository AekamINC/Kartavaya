Kray क्रय — Procurement Module

Proposal 85 · full rescope · 23 August 2026 · supersedes proposal 77's placement decision

A standalone procurement module covering the complete procure-to-pay (P2P) cycle for Indian SMBs: from purchase request through vendor selection, ordering, receiving, matching, and compliance. Modelled after ERPNext Buying, Zoho Books Purchases, and Odoo Purchase — adapted to the Indian CA/CS firm and mid-market contractor.

The P2P cycle — who owns what

Purchase Request → RFQ → Purchase Order → GRN (receipt) → Vendor Bill → Three-way match → Payment → Bank reconciliation

■ Kray owns   ■ Shared (appears in both)   ■ Ganit owns

The scope boundary: Kray owns everything from the decision to buy through receiving and matching. Vendor bills (payables) appear in both modules — Kray shows them in the procurement context (linked to POs, three-way match, GRNI) while Ganit shows them in the accounting context (ageing, payments, bank recon, cash flow). Vendor payments and bank reconciliation stay exclusively in Ganit.

Indian compliance — procurement-specific

TDS Section 194Q

  • Trigger: Buyer turnover > ₹10 crore preceding FY (org-level flag in settings — this product cannot verify it)
  • Threshold: ₹50 lakh cumulative purchase per vendor per FY
  • Rate: 0.1% on amount exceeding threshold (0.05% if vendor has filed ITR for 2 preceding years, per Finance Act 2024)
  • Kray's role: Track running cumulative per vendor. Warn at PO creation time (early signal). Report vendors approaching threshold. The actual deduction happens at bill/payment — that's Ganit.
  • What we show: Purchased YTD + on-order = projected total. Flag at 80% of threshold. Report with all vendors above warning level.

MSME Payment Compliance (Section 43B(h))

  • Rule: Payments to micro/small enterprises must happen within 15 days (no agreement) or 45 days (with written agreement) of acceptance of goods/services.
  • Consequence: Non-compliance = deduction for the expense disallowed, deferred to year of actual payment. Plus compound interest at 3× RBI bank rate (~24% currently).
  • Kray's role: Flag vendor as MSME (Udyam number). Track acceptance date (earliest receipt date, already implemented). Alert at configurable thresholds (default: 10, 30, 40 days). Weekly MSME ageing report.
  • Acceptance date source: Already implemented — record_receipt writes ganit_vendor_bills.acceptance_date from the earliest receipt. This is the statutory clock start.
  • Visual: MSME vendors get a badge. Overdue MSME bills highlighted red in payables. Dedicated MSME compliance report.

GST on purchases

  • Input Tax Credit (ITC): Every vendor bill captures CGST/SGST/IGST. Already implemented in bill creation.
  • IGST determination: derive_is_igst() already exists in services/purchase_orders.py. Inter-state = IGST, intra-state = CGST+SGST.
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): Unregistered vendor purchases attract RCM. Phase 2: flag on bill, auto-compute liability.
  • GSTR-2B reconciliation: Phase 3. Match vendor-filed invoices (from GST portal) against booked purchases. Surface mismatches.
  • Blocked credits (Section 17(5)): Phase 3. Flag expenses where ITC is not claimable (motor vehicles, food, personal use, etc.).

Other compliance hooks

  • HSN/SAC code on PO lines: Already present (inherited from product catalogue). Feeds into GSTR-1.
  • E-way bill: Phase 3. Required for goods movement > ₹50K. Not procurement-specific — belongs in a shared compliance layer.
  • Form 26Q / TDS returns: Ganit's concern (payment-side), but Kray provides the vendor PAN and TDS category data.

Budget & spend management

Currently budgets are a settings sub-panel with one report endpoint. This promotes them to a first-class feature.

Budget model

  • Budget dimension: Department (free text on employee record — the caveat the existing endpoint already states). Phase 2: budget by project or cost centre.
  • Budget period: Annual with optional monthly/quarterly distribution.
  • Three figures per budget line:
    1. Budget — the allocated amount
    2. Committed — POs issued but not yet billed (encumbrance). Drops when the bill arrives.
    3. Actual — billed amount (vendor bills linked to this department)
  • Available = Budget − Committed − Actual
  • Control action: Warn (alert but allow PO), Stop (block PO creation when budget exceeded), or Ignore. Configurable per department.
  • Tolerance: Allow X% overrun before stopping (default 10%).

Budget tab features

  • Department list with progress bars (committed + actual vs budget)
  • Colour coding: green (<80%), amber (80-100%), red (>100%)
  • Click to drill down: which POs and bills consume this budget
  • Set/edit budget limits inline
  • Monthly burn rate trend
  • Forecast: at current burn rate, when does this department exhaust its budget?

Approval workflow — tiered

The existing approval system names specific people. This proposal adds amount-based tiers (common in ERPNext and SAP):

TierAmount rangeApproversAuto-approve?
Routine< ₹10,000None (auto-approved on submit)Yes
Standard₹10K – ₹1L1 named approverNo
Material₹1L – ₹10L2 named approvers (sequential)No
High-value> ₹10LPartner/owner approval requiredNo

Thresholds and approvers are fully configurable per org in Settings. The tiers above are defaults. Existing single-tier approval rules continue to work unchanged — the tiered system is opt-in.

Shared-DB risk: Step 1 (entitlements) MUST complete before step 3 (backend deploy). If the backend gates on kray before the rows exist, the route 403s for everyone. The migration is additive INSERT — it cannot break existing behaviour.
Payables shared surface: Since payables appears in both modules, the backend endpoints for vendor bills do NOT move — they stay gated on ganit OR kray (either entitlement grants access). Implementation: _bill_gate = require_module_any("ganit", "kray").

  • ganit_purchase_orders, ganit_po_lines, ganit_po_receipts, ganit_po_approvals, ganit_po_revisions — owned by Kray
