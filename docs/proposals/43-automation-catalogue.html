Every automation is the same sentence: when this happens → check this → do that. You pick the three parts from a list. Nothing here needs code.

How you will build one

Three dropdowns and a save button.

When — an invoice becomes overdue.
If — the amount is over ₹50,000 and the customer is not on hold.
Then — WhatsApp the pay link, wait 7 days, then email the accounts contact, then tell me.

Four things every rule gets, whether you ask for them or not:

  • A test run — "what would this have done last month", answered before you switch it on.
  • An off switch — per rule, and one for everything at once.
  • A history — on the invoice, the deal, the employee. Not buried in a settings page.
  • A stop — chasing ends the moment the reason for chasing does.

Three rules I will hold it to

  1. No rule moves money. It can raise an invoice, chase a payment and tell you it arrived. It cannot pay anybody.
  2. No rule shouts. Everything respects the quiet hours and notification settings a person has already chosen. Automation that ignores those gets muted entirely, and then the important ones are muted too.
  3. Nothing says "sent" unless it was sent. If a message was suppressed or failed, the history says so.
