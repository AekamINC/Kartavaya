The page behind the QR

Every branded code on the invoice points here — https://pay.kartavaya.com/i/7fQ2x9?via=phonepe — not at a payment scheme. Any camera on any OS can open an https link, which is what makes one sticker work on an iPhone, an Android, an iPad and a Mac. This page then decides how to reach the app. Below is what the customer sees on each.

How each platform is reached

The page picks one of these. Nothing depends on guessing the OS from its name — it branches on whether the device can open apps and take touch input.

PlatformMechanismIf the app is missing
Android intent://pay?pa=…&am=1292782.50#Intent;scheme=phonepe;package=com.phonepe.app;S.browser_fallback_url=https%3A%2F%2Fpay.kartavaya.com%2Fi%2F7fQ2x9;end Android itself returns to the fallback URL. Silent, no error.
iPhone / iPad Sets location.href to the app scheme, and starts a timer. iOS shows its own confirm dialog. The timer fires, the page is still visible, and the app list and QR are shown.
Mac / Windows No scheme attempted. Renders the standard upi:// QR to be scanned by a phone. Not applicable — nothing to install.
Bank app scanner Scans the large code on the invoice, which is a real UPI QR, never this link. Not applicable.

What this still needs

Honest list, not a sales pitch.

A subdomainpay.kartavaya.com does not exist yet. The QR codes in this demo encode the real URL, so scanning one today gives a DNS error — the code is correct, the destination isn't built.
A public routeOne unauthenticated endpoint keyed by an opaque short token, rate-limited and expiring. It exposes payee, invoice number and amount — all of which are already printed on the invoice, so nothing new leaks. The invoice UUID is never in the URL.
Paid detectionThe page must refuse a settled invoice. A plain upi:// QR will happily collect the same money twice; this is the main safety argument for routing through a page at all.
No gatewayKartavaya never touches the money and takes on no PCI scope. The customer pays the org's VPA directly, exactly as they do today.
WhatsApp PayHas no documented deep link. Its row opens the generic UPI chooser, which is the honest behaviour rather than a button that silently does nothing.
