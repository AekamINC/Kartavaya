Those four charge nothing right now. Neither do scheduled skills — they run on a timer forever, free, while the same skill run by hand charges.

Why this is simpler than what exists

One wallet. There are two today. Every debit reads one; the only top-up form in the product writes the other. Topping up through the UI raises the spendable balance by zero.

Two buckets, one balance. The month-end reset overwrites the balance — so a top-up your client paid for is destroyed if they don't spend it by the 31st, and the ledger records it as routine.

A member cap, not a member wallet. One balance, nothing stranded, and it does what you actually want: stop one person burning the whole allowance.

One price list. Two exist and can't be reconciled — an image costs $0.0352 and charges 3 credits; the text beside it costs $0.0027 and charges 2.
