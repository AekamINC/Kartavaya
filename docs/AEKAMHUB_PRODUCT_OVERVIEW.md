## 1. Executive Summary

AekamHub is a white-labeled AI marketing portal that transforms Aekam Inc's social media marketing (SMM) service model from human-execution-dependent to AI-platform-powered. Each client gets a branded subdomain (e.g., `keyadesigns.kartavaya.com`), a pre-configured AI content generation engine tuned to their brand voice, and self-service tools to generate, approve, and publish marketing content — all powered by multi-provider AI APIs at near-zero marginal cost.

**Why now:** Aekam lost 5–6 SMM clients paying ₹15–20K/month because late-30s business owners discovered they could replicate execution-layer work with ChatGPT + Canva for ₹1,500/month. ## 2. The Problem — Why Agencies Are Losing Clients

### 2.1 The Industry Crisis

Agency churn is structural, not anecdotal. Delivery dissatisfaction is the #1 reason clients leave agencies in 2026, cited by 48% of departing clients — up 14 percentage points year over year. Retainer-based agencies average 18–22% annual churn. The first 90 days are the peak risk window.

Average agency revenue growth has dropped to 7.5%, well below historical norms. Only 39% of agencies grew at all in 2025. The market is shrinking while clients are getting harder to keep.

### 2.2 The AI In-Housing Wave

82% of small business employers have invested in AI tools, with marketing as the #1 use case. The median small business now uses five AI tools. For ₹1,500–₹3,000/month (ChatGPT Plus + Canva Pro + Buffer), a business owner can now generate social media captions, create professional graphics, schedule posts, and get analytics — all without an agency.

This is not theoretical. This is what Aekam's ex-clients are doing right now. The total monthly cost of the DIY stack is 10–15% of what they were paying Aekam.

### 2.3 Gujarat's Agency Saturation

Ahmedabad alone has 100+ digital marketing agencies. The pricing floor starts at ₹15,000/month for basic social media packages — exactly where Aekam sits. At this price point, agencies compete with solo freelancers, college students running "agencies" from co-working spaces, and AI-powered micro-agencies where one person manages 50+ accounts.

There is no defensible moat at ₹15–20K/month for execution-layer work.

### 2.4 Aekam's Specific Situation

Aekam lost 5–6 SMM clients in a short window. These were business owners in their late 30s, each paying ₹15,000–₹20,000/month for social media management, campaign/lead generation, and blog posting.

**Root causes (ranked by impact):**

1. AI tools made execution-layer work DIY-able for tech-savvy clients — *Critical*
2. No strategic value layer above execution — *Critical*
3. Pricing at the market floor with no differentiation — *High*
4. Opaque or insufficient reporting/attribution — *High*
5. No structural switching costs or IP lock-in — *Medium*
6. Peer-network churn cascade among similar-demographic clients — *Medium*
7. No formal onboarding, expectation setting, or check-in cadence — *Medium*

### 2.5 The Insight

These clients don't actually want to spend their evenings writing captions and designing posts. They want **control** and **value for money**. If Aekam builds a platform where clients log in, click a few buttons, and get on-brand content generated instantly — because Aekam has already loaded their brand DNA, industry knowledge, and content strategy into the system — you've created something they can't replicate with raw ChatGPT.

The switching cost isn't the software. The switching cost is **the months of brand configuration, trained agents, content strategy, and industry expertise that Aekam builds into each client's workspace**. Walk away from AekamHub and you lose all of that.

**One-liner:** A white-labeled AI marketing portal where Aekam configures the intelligence, and clients generate the output.

**Layer 1 — Aekam's Role (Setup & Ongoing Strategy):**
Aekam's team is the invisible engine. The client logs into their branded dashboard, clicks "Generate this week's Instagram posts," and gets 7 on-brand posts with captions and hashtags. They get the control and speed they wanted when they left Aekam, but with 10x better output because Aekam's brand intelligence is baked into every AI call.

If a client leaves AekamHub after 6 months, they lose:
- A brand voice engine that took weeks to configure and months to refine
- A trained chatbot that has captured hundreds of leads
- A content library with 6 months of generated and approved content
- Custom skill packs tuned to their industry and workflow
- Analytics history and performance data
- Their approval workflow setup and editorial calendar

Replicating this on ChatGPT would take them months and still wouldn't match the depth. This is the moat.

## 7. Technical Architecture

### 7.1 Multi-Tenant Subdomain Architecture

**One app, infinite clients.** AekamHub is deployed as a single React application on Vercel with a single FastAPI backend on Railway. Cloudflare DNS has one wildcard record: `*.kartavaya.com → Vercel`. No per-client infrastructure, no per-client deployments, no per-client DNS changes.

**New client onboarding is one database row:**
1. Aekam admin creates client in admin panel (name, slug, brand colors, logo)
2. System inserts one row in `hub_clients` table
3. `{slug}.kartavaya.com` works immediately — no deployment, no config change
4. Infrastructure cost for new client: ₹0

**Request flow:**
1. Client visits `keyadesigns.kartavaya.com`
2. React app reads hostname, extracts slug `keyadesigns`
3. Calls `/api/hub/config/keyadesigns` — gets brand config (colors, logo, name)
4. App renders entirely in client's brand
5. All subsequent API calls include client context
6. FastAPI middleware resolves slug → `client_id` → `org_id`
7. RLS ensures data isolation — client can only see their own data

### 7.2 Data Isolation

PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database engine level. Every table has `client_id` column with RLS policy: `WHERE client_id = current_setting('app.current_client_id')::uuid`. Client A's queries physically cannot return Client B's rows — even if application code has a bug, the database itself blocks cross-client access.

Aekam admin uses `service_role` key which bypasses RLS — sees all data across all clients.

### 7.3 Authentication

Supabase Auth handles all login/signup:
- **Client users:** Email/password login, scoped to their workspace
- **Aekam admins:** Email/password with admin role, access to admin dashboard
- **Roles:** `client_owner`, `client_member`, `aekam_admin`, `aekam_strategist`

### 7.4 Technology Stack

| Component | Technology | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Frontend | React 19 (CRA + CRACO + Tailwind) | Client portal + Admin dashboard |
| Hosting | Vercel (Pro plan) | CDN, wildcard subdomain |

| Compute | Railway (1 vCPU, 1GB RAM) | Backend hosting |

## 8. Infrastructure & Compute

### 8.1 How Compute Works

The critical insight: **AekamHub is a lightweight API proxy, not a compute-heavy application.** When a client generates content, FastAPI does near-zero work — it builds a prompt (microseconds), calls an external AI API (Google/Groq compute), and saves the result. There is no ML inference, no image processing, no heavy computation on your servers.

**Request timeline for "Generate Instagram Post":**
- Browser loads static React from Vercel CDN: 0ms compute
- FastAPI receives request, checks credits, fetches brand profile: ~10ms
- FastAPI calls Gemini API: 2–5 seconds (Google's compute, not yours)
- FastAPI saves result to Supabase: ~5ms
- Total your compute: ~15ms. Total wait: 2–5 seconds (90% is AI thinking)

### 8.2 Concurrency

50 users across 10 clients does not mean 50 simultaneous API calls. Most user time is spent browsing the dashboard, reviewing content, and editing. Realistic peak concurrent AI generation requests: 5–8 at any moment.

FastAPI with uvicorn is fully async — while waiting for Gemini's response (2–5 seconds), the CPU is free to serve other requests. A single 1 vCPU + 1GB Railway instance handles 100+ concurrent async connections easily.

## 9. AI Strategy — Multi-Provider, Credit-Based

### 9.1 Multi-Provider Architecture

AekamHub is not locked into any single AI provider. The backend has an AI routing layer (`ai_service.py`) that selects the optimal model per task:

| Task Type | Primary Model | Fallback | Why |
|-----------|--------------|----------|-----|
| Social posts, captions | Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | Llama 3.3 via Groq | Cheapest, fast, good enough |
| Blog posts, long-form | Gemini 2.5 Flash | Mistral Small 4 | Better reasoning for quality content |
| Ad copy variants | Gemini 2.5 Flash | Groq Llama 3.3 | Needs creativity + speed |
| Chatbot conversations | Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | Groq Llama 3.2 | Cheapest for high volume |
| Image analysis | Gemini Flash (vision) | Mistral Medium 3 | Multimodal required |
| Email campaigns | Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | Llama 3.3 via Groq | Simple, high volume |

**Alternative: OpenRouter** — single API key routes to 400+ models with auto-failover. 5.5% markup but zero vendor lock-in. If any provider raises prices or goes down, change one model string — zero code change.

**Why not Claude API:** Indian SMB clients won't absorb premium AI costs. Gemini Flash-Lite at $0.10/$0.40 per 1M tokens is 50x cheaper than Claude Sonnet. At AekamHub's volume, the quality difference for social media content is negligible.

## Appendix A: Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Brand Intelligence Engine | The AI configuration layer where Aekam loads a client's brand voice, visual identity, audience, and content strategy |
| Credit | Abstract unit of AI usage. Each generation action costs a fixed number of credits. Not tied to rupees or tokens. |
| RLS | Row Level Security — PostgreSQL feature that enforces data isolation at the database engine level |
| RAG | Retrieval-Augmented Generation — AI technique that retrieves relevant documents before generating a response |
| pgvector | PostgreSQL extension for storing and searching vector embeddings (used for chatbot knowledge base) |
| Skill Pack | Pre-built AI workflow for specific use cases (festival content, product launch campaigns, etc.) |
| Slug | URL-safe identifier for a client (e.g., "keyadesigns" in keyadesigns.kartavaya.com) |
| Multi-tenant | Architecture where one application serves multiple clients with isolated data |
| Top-up | Additional credit pack purchased beyond monthly allocation |
