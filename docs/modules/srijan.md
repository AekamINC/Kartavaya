# Srijan सृजन — AI assistant and skills

**Module code** `srijan` · registered in `backend/middleware/role_tiers.py`

Generation against the org's own data, priced in credits. Runs skills — social posts, ad copy, email campaigns, GST answers — plus a grounded chatbot and the Apify scraper catalogue.

## Flow

A run debits credits, calls a model through the router in `services/ai_router.py`, records what it touched and what it cost in `hub_ai_logs`, and refunds on failure. Every run states its spend; nothing is silently billed.
